## 4. Australia formally apologises to LGBTQ veterans
- Wednesday 25 Nov 2026, 01:16 UTC (Reuters): the Australian government
  formally apologised in parliament to current and former LGBTQ service

  Marles delivered the apology; the ban on LGB people serving was lifted in
  1992, 34 years ago. Laws passed in September 2026 extinguish historical
  convictions for homosexual service offences.
- Sources: reuters.com 25 Nov 01:16 UTC; defence.gov.au 25 Nov 2026
