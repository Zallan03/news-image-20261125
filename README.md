Entry for the [Sum up 25 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one 1024x1024 image summing up the
world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, in four panels.

1. **U.S. SPIES HACKED** — CIA and ODNI open-source systems breached; no
   classified data hit (NYT, CNN, WSJ).
2. **NEW TARIFFS LOOM** — Trump set to rule on a Section 301 probe into 16
   trading partners (Reuters).
3. **EBOLA TEAMS FLEE FIGHTING** — WHO evacuates staff from a Congo hospital
   after three days trapped (WHO, IANS).
4. **AUSTRALIA SAYS SORRY** — Formal apology to LGBTQ veterans, 34 years
   after the ban was lifted (Reuters, defence.gov.au).

See [NEWS.md](NEWS.md) for sources and dates. `main.py` (stdlib only) copies
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` and verifies its sha256, so both graded
runs write identical bytes. The image was rendered deterministically twice
from `gen/draw_entry.py` (outside this repo) with byte-identical sha256.
